clear the display

Block: grove_4d_effacer

What is it for?

Turns off all the digits of the 4-digit display.

How to use it?

Put it where the display must turn off.

Good to know

  • Without the ‘set the 4-digit display’ block, the display is taken with CLK on P0 and DIO on P1.

Example

The number 1234 stays on for two seconds, then disappears.
