convert … from degrees to radians

Block: math_convert

What is it for?

Converts an angle from degrees to radians, or from radians to degrees.

This block returns a number (to plug into another block, where a number is expected, or into a comparison block = < >).

How to use it?

Attach the angle, then choose the direction of the conversion.

Good to know

  • 180° is π radians (about 3.14).

Example
